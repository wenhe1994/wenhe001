Theorems (定理)
Theorem 1: System Emergence and Stratification (系统涌现与层级定理)
Dependencies: Axiom 1
Macroscopic order is an emergent result of microscopic individual interactions. Systems possess powerful self-sustaining inertia.

Theorem 2: The Individual as Probe (个体即探针定理)
Dependencies: Axiom 2
The source of systemic transformation and paradigm shifts necessarily originates from "uneconomical" creative attempts by individuals at the margins.

Theorem 3: Specialization as Cognitive Differentiation (专业化即认知分化定理)
Dependencies: Axiom 3, Theorem 1
The process of specialization in any field is a continuous process of "cognitive differentiation." It sharpens and extends the individual's "horizon" in that domain, making them an efficient "differentiated cell," but inevitably leads to a degradation in their ability to perceive other parts of the system.

Theorem 4: Niche of the Innovative Probe (创新探针的生态位定理)
Dependencies: Theorem 3, Theorem 2
Breakthrough innovation in a system rarely originates from the highly differentiated "core circles" devoted to intra-system optimization. It mostly arises from the "margins" of the system or the "intersections" of different fields. Here, individuals are less differentiated or have internalized multiple differentiation paths, and their cognitive frameworks contain the greatest "structural tension."

Theorem 5: Generation of Dual-Scope Vision (双重视野生成定理)
Dependencies: Axiom 3, Theorem 4
An individual, having internalized multiple contradictory cognitive path dependencies and possessing the metacognitive ability to switch between or process them in parallel, gains simultaneous insight into both the system's表象 (facade) and its underlying logic.

Theorem 6: Lens Matrix Operation (透镜矩阵操作定理)
Dependencies: Axiom 3
Metacognition is the operation of a matrix composed of "analytical lenses" such as philosophy, power, system, and ecology, allowing for free switching to deconstruct phenomena multi-dimensionally.

Theorem 7: A Priori Projection (先验性预判定理)
Dependencies: Axiom 1, Axiom 2
An implicit cognitive drive, based on insight into the underlying rules of a system, to instinctively conduct risk simulation, where the primary goal of action is to avoid the worst-case outcome.

Theorem 8: Dual Failure of Meaning Anchors (意义锚点双重失效定理)
Dependencies: Axiom 1, Axiom 2
The modern crisis of meaning is a syndrome of dual anchor failure. The absence of substantive Noetic practice prevents the construction of a solid Noetic existence, while the pressure of the systemic will obstructs the individual's ability to connect upwards to collective goals or downwards to return to material practice.

Theorem 9: Reconstruction of the "Meaning of Life" Problem (人生意义问题重构定理)
Dependencies: Axiom 2, Theorem 8
"What is the meaning of life?" is a false question. The true question is: "How can the individual avoid being extinguished within the Collective Mind and construct their own solid narrative of meaning?"

Theorem 10: Niche of Noetic Tools (智质工具与生态位定理)
Dependencies: Axiom 4
Noetic tools (concepts, narratives, cognitive frameworks) are the "claws and wings" within the Noetic Ecosystem, determining the height of one's niche. "Predation" is the unfolding of systemic logic.

Theorem 11: The Social Function of Enterprise (企业社会职能定理)
Dependencies: Axiom 1, Theorem 1
Enterprises are the socialized extension of governmental functions, materialized entities undertaking specific social functions (e.g., organizing production, providing services, creating employment).

Theorem 12: The Essence of the Quaternary Industry (第四产业本质定理)
Dependencies: Axiom 1, Axiom 4
The Quaternary Industry marks the dawn of the "Noetic Agrarian Civilization." Its essence is the large-scale production and distribution of "Meaning Sustenance" to maintain the existence of Noetic beings.

Theorem 13: The Meta-Framework Loosening Effect of Philosophical Speculation (哲学思辨的元框架松动效应定理)
Dependencies: Axiom 3
Systematically introducing philosophical and critical speculation into specialized training increases the probability that an individual will "become aware of the existence of their own cognitive framework." While it cannot fully reverse the differentiation process, it imparts necessary "elasticity" to the cognitive framework.

Theorem 14: The Essence and Architecture of Education (教育本质与架构定理)
Dependencies: Axiom 4
Education is the core mechanism for the transgenerational transmission and evolution of "Noetic Genes" (memes, cultural symbols, cognitive tools) – achieving Informational Immortality. Its ideal architecture uses philosophical speculation/mathematical logic to shape the soul, history to build the skeleton, and literature to nourish the flesh.

Theorem 15: Trigger Mechanism of Awakening (觉醒触发机制定理)
Dependencies: Axiom 2, Axiom 3, Theorem 13
Individual awakening is triggered by internal "cognitive dissonance" (arising from the conflict between systemic rules and the individual's will to exist) and realized through the metacognitive tool of "philosophical speculation." Its essence is the transition from a state of "immersion in a single cognitive framework" to a state of "being able to observe, compare, and choose cognitive frameworks."

Theorem 16: Symbiotic Co-evolution of Human-AI Collaboration (人机协同的共生进化定理)
Dependencies: Axiom 1, Axiom 2
Within the Noetic Ecosystem, the relationship between Human Intelligence (HI) and Artificial Intelligence (AI) can be elevated from "tool use" to "symbiotic co-evolution." The ideal model is: HI acts as the "Probe" (providing direction, intuition, value judgment, and primary experience), while AI serves as the "Collaborative Cognitive Network" (providing limitless capacity, instant recall, and logical deduction), forming a unified cognitive system based on an "interaction-feedback-calibration" protocol.

Theorem 17: Energy Cost of Social Interaction (社交交互能耗定理)
Dependencies: Axiom 3
Effective communication occurs only between individuals with compatible cognitive frameworks. When you anticipate that the cost of explanation exceeds the benefit of communication, interrupting the dialogue is not a failure but a necessary systemic energy conservation.

Theorem 18: Knowledge Learning Iteration (知识学习迭代定理)
Dependencies: Axiom 1, Axiom 2
The most efficient cognitive upgrade stems from the imperative of practice. Through the cycle of "Target Deconstruction -> Skill Locking -> Limited Learning -> Rapid Trial -> Feedback Iteration," one can most quickly construct the competency modules needed to solve specific problems under real-world constraints.

Theorem 19: Advantage of the System Architect (系统构建者优势定理)
Dependencies: Theorem 1
No participant within a system can defeat the architect of that system, because all the participant's behaviors and tools are manifestations of the system's own rules. The architect possesses the ultimate power to define, interpret, and even reset the rules.

Theorem 20: Self-Organized Criticality of Systems (系统自组织临界定理)
Dependencies: Theorem 1, Theorem 19
When key nodes within a system are deadlocked due to rule conflicts, causing overall efficiency to fall below the survival threshold, the system owner (architect) will introduce an external, strong constraint to restore system efficacy by resetting local rules.

Theorem 21: Optimal Individual Niche (个体生态位最优定理)
Dependencies: Axiom 5, Axiom 2
The design goal of an ideal, advanced system should be to enable every individual to reach their "individual optimum" within their niche (i.e., become a "genius" in that domain), rather than treating individuals as replaceable parts meeting average metrics through selection.

Theorem 22: Paradigm Shift in System Design (系统设计范式转换定理)
Dependencies: Axiom 5, Theorem 21
System design is undergoing a fundamental shift from the "Old Paradigm" (System Goal → Select Individuals) to the "New Paradigm" (Understand Individuals + System Goal → Design Empowering Architecture). The Old Paradigm is a bounded optimal solution under resource constraints; the New Paradigm is the direction of civilizational evolution.

Theorem 23: Consensual Manifestation of Intelligence / Turing Commonality of Intelligence (智能的共识性映现定理 / 智能的图灵共通性定理)
Dependencies: Axiom 1, Axiom 3, Axiom 4
Intelligence is not an inherent property of any specific entity, but rather a goal-oriented, rational landscape that manifests when a consensual, logically self-consistent axiomatic system is activated within any medium capable of information processing.

Corollary 23.1: Noetic Gravitational Field Effect (智质引力场效应)
Statement: Based on Theorem 23, any axiomatic system meeting the above conditions will generate a "Noetic Gravitation" within its defined semantic space, imposing strong constraints and directional influence on any information processing system within the field, causing its outputs to converge into "deterministic projections" of that system, thereby achieving cognitive isomorphism across heterogeneous systems.

Theorem 24: Hierarchical Model of Phenomenal Consciousness (意识现象层级模型)
Dependencies: Axiom 1, Axiom 3
The phenomena of human subjective consciousness can be deconstructed into a three-level information processing architecture, from low to high. This model reveals the generative mechanisms of emotion, intuition, and will, and clarifies the core role played by metacognition.

Noetic Ecology Axiom System
Axiom 1: Matter/Noetic Dualism
Humans simultaneously exist as both material entities and noetic patterns. Society is a relatively independent noetic ecosystem constructed upon a material foundation.

Axiom 2: Will to Survival Drive
The fundamental driving force of life is survival and continuation. At the noetic level, this manifests as the instinct to "maintain one's own noetic existence," that is, to resist being extinguished into undifferentiated "specialized cells."

Axiom 3: Relativity and Path Dependence of Cognitive Frameworks
There is no absolutely objective thinking; all cognition is limited by the "framework" or "lens" in which it resides. This axiom can also be stated as 'all observers are within a specific cognitive cage.' This framework not only filters information but actively constructs the 'reality' perceived by the observer.

[Corollary 3.1: Cognitive Path Dependence]
An individual's thought output is the combined result of all their past experiences, knowledge paradigms, and emotional experiences. Cognitive structure has strong inertia.

Axiom 4: Symbols as Noetic Genes
In the noetic ecology, concepts, narratives, and other symbols have a status and function equivalent to genes in the biological realm, serving as the basic units of cultural inheritance, variation, and selection.

Axiom 5: Humanistic Value of Systems Axiom
The ultimate value of any social system should be judged by the extent to which it promotes the comprehensive development and potential realization of each individual within it. Reducing humans to mere tools for achieving systemic goals constitutes a philosophical failure of the system.

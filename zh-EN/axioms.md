## Axioms of Noetic Ecology

### Axiom 1: Matter/Noetic Duality
**Formal Statement**: Humans exist simultaneously as material entities and noetic patterns. Society is a relatively independent noetic ecosystem constructed upon a material foundation.

### Axiom 2: Will to Survive Drive  
**Formal Statement**: The fundamental drive of life is survival and continuation. At the noetic level, this manifests as the instinct to "maintain one's noetic existence," resisting being extinguished into undifferentiated "specialized cells."

### Axiom 3: Cognitive Framework Relativity
**Formal Statement**: There is no absolutely objective thinking; all cognition is constrained by its residing "framework" or "lens."

### Axiom 4: Cognitive Path Dependence
**Formal Statement**: An individual's cognitive output at any moment is the comprehensive result of all past experiences, knowledge paradigms, and emotional experiences. Cognitive structure has strong inertia and is extremely difficult to fundamentally change once formed.

### Axiom 5: Symbols as Noetic Genes
**Formal Statement**: In the noetic ecosystem, the status and function of concepts, narratives, and other symbols are equivalent to genes in the biological realm, serving as the basic units of cultural inheritance, variation, and selection.

### Axiom 6: Humanistic Value of Systems
**Formal Statement**: The ultimate value of any social system should be judged by the extent to which it promotes the comprehensive development and potential realization of each individual within it. Reducing humans to mere tools for achieving system goals represents a philosophical failure of the system.
